# Flight audio sources

Rotor, engine, rocket motor and debris impact recordings were selected from the user-supplied Gunship Battle APK extraction. They are separate from the CC0 and CC BY visual assets. Rotor, engine and motor files were shortened by 80 ms with an equal-power end/start crossfade to make loops; the debris recording is unchanged. These are game recordings, not new field recordings or an assertion of recording authenticity.
